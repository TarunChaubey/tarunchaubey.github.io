Customer Support & Operations Copilot

Human approval

A workflow-driven assistant combining enterprise retrieval, API/tool execution, and task orchestration to support customer service and internal operations.

LangGraphTool callingRAGAWS
How the workflow works
  1. IntakeClassify intent and identify the requested outcome.
  2. PlanDecompose the request into retrieval and action steps.
  3. GroundRetrieve policy, troubleshooting, and customer context.
  4. ActSelect and validate the required enterprise tools.
  5. ApprovePause for human review when the action is high impact.
  6. RecoverRetry safely, use fallbacks, and preserve state for resume.
